How to Request Account & Data Deletion

If you wish to delete your Tickora CRM account and all associated personal and organizational data, please follow the steps below:

  1. Send an email to finytiveprivatelimited@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request - Tickora CRM".
  2. Include your registered email address and Organization name in the email body.
  3. Our support team will process your request and permanently delete your account and associated data within 7 business days.

1. Data Retention & Deletion Policy

When an account deletion request is processed, the following data types are permanently removed from our active servers:

  • User profile credentials, full name, and contact details.
  • Lead records, customer contact notes, and sales pipeline entries.
  • Uploaded documents, attachments, and call recording metadata.

2. Data Encryption & Security

All data transmitted between the Tickora CRM mobile app and our servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS protocols.
